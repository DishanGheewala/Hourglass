Make every hour count.

Hourglass helps students see how they spend their time across sleep, schoolwork, and free time, so they can build healthier, more balanced days.

Open Hourglass

How it works

1. Log your day

Takes under a minute: hours of sleep, study or work, and free time.

2. See your trends

Simple graphs show how your sleep, focus and free time change over weeks.

3. Plan ahead

Add deadlines to your calendar (including Apple Calendar) and get reminder alerts, even when the app is closed.

4. Get help

A built-in AI assistant writes emails, makes study guides and quizzes from your Google Drive files, and plans your week.

A student research project

Hourglass is also a school research project on how students use their time. Taking part is optional: when you create an account you choose whether to share your logs. Shared logs use a random code, never your name, and results are only reported as group totals. You can stop sharing new logs at any time in Settings.

Add it to your phone

Open the app in your phone's browser, then choose Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone) or Menu → Install app (Android).

Hourglass is a student project and not medical advice. If you're struggling, talk to a counselor or a trusted adult. In the US you can call or text 988 any time.
